## Before You Begin

You'll need:

- Your email address (used for your Newcore account)
- Access to your email inbox for verification
- a bag of chips
- chicken hamburger

## Accept Your Invitation

1. Check your email for the Newcore invitation.
2. Click the **Get Started** or **Activate Account** link.
3. You'll be taken to the account setup page.

## Set Your Password

1. Create a password that meets your organization's requirements:
   - Minimum length (typically 8+ characters)
   - Mix of uppercase and lowercase letters
   - At least one number
   - Special character (if required)

2. Confirm your password.
3. Click **Continue**.

## Enroll MFA

If required by your organization, you'll be prompted to enroll multi-factor authentication:

### Mobile App (Recommended)

1. Download the Newcore app from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Open the app and tap **Pair Device**.
3. Scan the QR code displayed on screen.
4. Follow the prompts to complete enrollment.

### Authenticator App

1. Open your authenticator app (Google Authenticator, etc.).
2. Scan the QR code.
3. Enter the 6-digit code to verify.

### Other Methods

Your organization may offer additional options:
- SMS OTP
- WhatsApp OTP
- Security key

## Complete Setup

1. Review your profile information.
2. Click **Complete Setup**.
3. You're now logged in to the User Portal.

## Access Applications

After setup, you can:

1. View your assigned applications in the App Catalog.
2. Click any application to launch it via SSO.
3. Access the Admin Console if you have admin privileges.
